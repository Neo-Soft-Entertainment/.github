## About Us

Neo Soft Entertainment is an indie game studio founded in Brazil in **2020**, working remotely with a focus on the Brazilian dev scene. We combine strong Unreal Engine engineering with game feel and design to build original games, plugins, and production tools that help creators ship better and faster.

Our small, dedicated team is committed to delivering high-quality, production-ready solutions — whether it's our own games or systems built for other studios.

> **Studio manifesto:** systems with soul. Prototype fast, ship clean.

## Our Process

We follow a five-phase production loop for every build, ours or a client's:

1. **Signal** — define the fantasy, player promise, and technical risk
2. **Prototype** — build the smallest playable proof to validate feel and direction
3. **Systemize** — turn discoveries into durable gameplay components and tools
4. **Polish** — shape readability, feedback, camera behavior, and deployment paths
5. **Launch** — package, ship, monitor, and keep the product alive
